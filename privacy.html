Post Lab ("the app") is a desktop application made by GrampsHD that schedules and publishes videos to the YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts a user connects. This policy explains what information the app accesses and how it is handled.

Information the app accesses

  • Video files and post details you choose: the video files you select on your computer, and the titles, descriptions, captions, privacy choices and posting times you enter.
  • YouTube (Google): with your permission, the app uses the youtube.upload scope to upload videos you schedule, and the youtube.readonly scope to read your channel's name and ID so it can show which channel is connected.
  • Instagram (Meta): your Instagram professional account ID and username, and permission to publish the Reels you schedule.
  • TikTok: your TikTok display name and username (Login Kit, user.info.basic), and permission to publish the videos you schedule (video.publish), including the privacy options TikTok allows for your account.
  • Access tokens: the sign-in tokens each platform issues so the app can post on your behalf.

How information is used

The information above is used only to upload and publish the videos you schedule, to show you which accounts are connected, and to show you the result of each post (for example, a link to the published video or an error message). The app does not use your information for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

Where information is stored

All data stays on your own computer. Your queue, settings and activity log are stored in the app's local data folder, and access tokens are stored encrypted using your operating system's built-in protection (Windows DPAPI). The app has no servers. No information is sent to GrampsHD or to any third party other than the platform you are posting to (Google/YouTube, Meta/Instagram or TikTok), and only to perform the post you requested.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Video files and post details are sent only to the platform you chose to post to, through that platform's official API.

Google API Services

Post Lab's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Post Lab uses YouTube API Services; by connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Information is kept on your computer until you delete it. You can disconnect an account in the app (which deletes its stored tokens), remove posts from the queue, or uninstall the app and delete its data folder. You can also revoke access at any time: Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, Meta in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations, and TikTok in Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Children

Post Lab is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
