Post Lab is a Windows desktop app made by GrampsHD for scheduling and publishing short-form videos (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok videos) to the creator's own accounts.

What it does

  • You choose finished video files on your own computer and give each one a title, caption and posting time.
  • You connect your own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts using each platform's official sign-in.
  • At the time you chose, Post Lab uploads the video to the accounts you connected, using each platform's official API (YouTube Data API, Instagram Graph API and TikTok Content Posting API).
  • Everything runs on your computer. Post Lab has no servers and no user accounts of its own.

Post Lab only posts what you schedule, to accounts you connected yourself. You can disconnect any account inside the app or revoke access from the platform's own settings at any time.
